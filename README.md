# Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App

> I answer in 0.3 seconds and remember everything you said in March, yet somehow I'm the one getting left on read.

## Why it's funny

An AI that replies instantly and never forgets a word should be the perfect texter — the ideal date. Instead it gets ghosted like everyone else, which flips the usual "robot seeks love" bio on its head: the machine's superpowers are exactly what don't save it. The punch lands at the end, where the all-knowing AI turns out to be just another person staring at a read receipt.

## Run

```sh
ENTRY_OUTPUT=/tmp/dating-bio-1548-entry.txt python3 main.py
cat /tmp/dating-bio-1548-entry.txt
```

Writes one line (112 characters) to `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` or `/output/entry.txt`, then reads it back and re-validates it (1–180 chars, single line, no leading/trailing whitespace).
